About Us

Building India's Tech-Ready Generation

Coding Arena is a forward-thinking EdTech startup on a mission to bridge the gap between school education and real-world technology. We believe every student — regardless of background — deserves access to the skills that will define the next century.

We tie up directly with schools and deliver structured, age-appropriate programming and entrepreneurship education from Class 6 through Class 12. Our curriculum isn't just theory — students build real websites, train AI models, and even launch their own startups under expert guidance.

  • 🎯
    School Partnerships We work directly with school administrations to integrate our program seamlessly
  • 📚
    Structured Modules Professionally prepared curriculum materials for every class and topic
  • 🌐
    Expert Network Students get access to our wide network of industry professionals and mentors
  • 🚀
    Real Launch Support We help students actually launch their websites, models, and businesses

    How It Works

    Simple. Seamless. Impactful.

    Getting Coding Arena into your school is a straightforward process — we handle everything.

    1

    School Reaches Out

    A principal, teacher, or administrator contacts us through this website or directly to express interest.

    2

    Discovery Meeting

    We schedule a meeting with the school leadership to understand needs, discuss the curriculum, and answer questions.

    3

    Student Seminar

    We conduct an engaging introductory seminar for students to spark excitement and help them understand the journey ahead.

    4

    Deliver Sessions

    Regular structured sessions begin, with our instructors delivering the curriculum, projects, and mentorship throughout the year.
